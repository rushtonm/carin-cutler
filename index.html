About

A general counsel's judgment, available fractionally.

Carin Cutler is a corporate and securities attorney who has spent her career at the center of company-defining transactions. Most recently she served as Vice President and General Counsel of ION Clean Energy, where she owned the full legal function of a growth-stage company, from financings and commercial contracts to governance and day-to-day risk.

Before stepping in-house, she was a partner at Ballard Spahr and, earlier, at Hogan Lovells and the boutique corporate practice Gross Cutler Seiler Dupont. Across those roles she has represented companies and investors in equity and debt financings by venture capital, private equity, and strategic investors; mergers, acquisitions, and recapitalizations; and the full lifecycle of an emerging company, from formation through acquisition or IPO.

She now offers that experience on a fractional basis: embedded legal leadership for portfolio companies and funds that need seasoned counsel at the table, scaled to what the business actually requires.
