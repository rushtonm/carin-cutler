About Inline Law

A general counsel's judgment, available fractionally.

Carin Cutler is a corporate and securities attorney who has spent her career advising companies and investors on a full range of legal issues emerging companies face throughout their lifecycle, always taking a practical, business-oriented approach. She most recently served as Vice President and General Counsel of ION Clean Energy, where she owned the full legal function of a growth-stage company, from financings and commercial contracts to governance and day-to-day risk.

Before stepping in-house, she was a partner at Ballard Spahr and, earlier, at Hogan Lovells and the boutique corporate practice Gross Cutler Seiler Dupont. Across those roles she has represented companies and investors in equity and debt financings by venture capital, private equity, and strategic investors; mergers, acquisitions, and recapitalizations; and the full lifecycle of an emerging, high growth company, from formation through acquisition or other exit.

She now offers that experience through Inline Law, on a fractional basis: embedded legal leadership for portfolio companies that need seasoned counsel at the table, scaled to what the business actually requires. She runs a modern practice built on secure technology, using vetted AI tools to deliver senior-level work with the speed and cost efficiency a growing company needs.

Approach
