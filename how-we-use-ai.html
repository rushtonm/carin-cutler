How Inline Law uses AI.

Inline Law uses secure, enterprise generative AI tools to assist with tasks such as drafting, summarizing and comparing documents, and legal research. These tools help us work efficiently. They do not replace a lawyer's professional judgment.

Lawyer review

Carin Cutler reviews and verifies all AI-assisted work, including any legal authority it cites, and remains fully responsible for the advice and work product clients receive.

Confidentiality

Client confidential information is entered only into AI tools whose terms prohibit the provider from using inputs or outputs to train its models or disclosing them to others, except as needed to provide the service or as required by law, and whose confidentiality and security protections have been reviewed for consistency with our professional obligations. Client information is never entered into consumer AI tools that do not meet these standards.

Transparency and client control

Our use of AI is described in each engagement letter. Clients may limit or prohibit the use of AI tools for any matter or category of information at any time, and we follow the AI and information-security policies our clients provide to us.

Fees

Clients are billed only for time actually spent on their matters. We do not bill for time spent learning to use AI tools, and the cost of general AI subscriptions is practice overhead that is not charged to clients.

Questions about our use of AI are welcome at carin@inlinelaw.com.
